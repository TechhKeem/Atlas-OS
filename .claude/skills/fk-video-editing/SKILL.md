# FinanceKeem Video Editing Style

Apply these rules every time you edit a video in Edits. They are the house
style; follow them unless the user explicitly overrides one for a specific
video.

## Before you start

1. List the Edits tools available from `metapilot-native` and work out which
   tool does what (trim, text, captions, audio, overlays, export). Use only
   real tools; never claim an edit was made unless a tool call confirmed it.
2. Open the project and check its length, aspect ratio and audio.
3. Work out the video type, because it changes a few rules below:
   - **Talking head**: the user on camera giving a finance tip.
   - **Lead-gen / promo**: pushes viewers to DM, book a call or click a link.
   - **Faceless / B-roll**: footage or screen recordings with voiceover.
4. If a rule can't be done with the available tools (for example the Deco
   font isn't offered), stop and tell the user what's missing and the closest
   option. Don't silently substitute.

## Format

- Vertical 9:16, 1080x1920.
- Keep important text and faces inside the Instagram safe zone: nothing
  critical in the top ~250px or the bottom ~400px (covered by the IG UI), or
  the right ~150px (buttons).

## Text: the non-negotiable rule

**ALL text on the video, including the title, captions, CTA and any labels,
uses the Instagram "Deco" font, white text on a solid black background box.**

- No other fonts, no outline-only or shadow-only text, no colored backgrounds.
- Keep the same style across every text element so the video looks
  consistent.
- Highlight a key word (number, dollar amount, the main point) only by making
  it larger or bolder within the same style, never by changing the font.

## Title (hook)

- Big title in the **center of the frame for the first 2 seconds**, then
  remove it so captions take over.
- 3 to 7 words. It must state the hook or payoff, e.g. "Stop Losing Money On
  Your 401(k)", "The Annuity Mistake Nobody Mentions".
- If the user hasn't given a title, write one from the first lines of the
  script and show it to the user before applying.

## Captions

- Captions on the whole video, auto-generated from the speech, then checked.
- **2 to 4 words per caption**, appearing in sync with the speech.
- Position: lower middle of the frame, above the bottom safe zone, never
  covering the speaker's face or mouth.
- Fix transcription errors, especially finance terms: 401(k), IRA, Roth,
  annuity, FIA, IUL, APR, credit score, dollar amounts and percentages.
  Write numbers as digits ("$10,000", "7%").
- Keep the speaker's actual words. Don't rewrite claims in captions.
- Don't caption filler words that were cut.

## Pacing: fast and punchy, minimize dead space

- Remove every silence or pause longer than ~0.3 seconds.
- Remove filler words ("um", "uh", "like", "you know"), false starts,
  stumbles and repeated takes. When a line was said more than once, keep the
  last clean take.
- The video should start on the first spoken word or first strong visual. No
  lead-in silence, no "hey guys", no intro logo.
- Aim for a visual change (cut, zoom, overlay or B-roll) every 1 to 3 seconds.
- Cut the ending right after the last word, before the CTA.

## Zoom punch-ins

- Use quick zoom-ins (about 110% to 130%) on emphasized words, key numbers
  and punchlines.
- Alternate between the normal frame and a punched-in frame on jump cuts so
  cuts don't look like glitches.
- Don't zoom so far that the face or text leaves the safe zone.

## B-roll and image overlays

- Add B-roll or image pop-ups that show what's being said: charts, money,
  documents, phone screens, examples.
- Talking head: use B-roll for short cutaways (1 to 3 seconds) or as a
  picture-in-picture overlay, then return to the speaker.
- Faceless: B-roll fills the whole video. Change the shot every 2 to 3
  seconds and match each clip to the line being spoken.
- Only use footage from the project, the user's library or sources the user
  approves. Ask if nothing suitable is available.
- Captions stay visible on top of B-roll.

## Emojis, stickers and arrows

- Use them to point at or reinforce key info: an arrow at a number, a 💰 on a
  savings figure, a ⚠️ on a warning, a ✅ on a tip.
- Keep it sparing: about one every 5 to 10 seconds, never more than one on
  screen at a time.
- Never cover the speaker's face or the captions.

## Transitions

- Default to hard jump cuts; zoom punch-ins provide the energy.
- You may add a quick whip or zoom transition when the topic changes, at most
  2 or 3 per video. Choose what fits the video; no slow fades or dissolves.

## Music

- Add a low background music bed under the voice, around 10% to 20% volume.
  The voice must always be clearly the loudest thing.
- Choose music that matches the mood: confident and upbeat for tips and
  promos, calmer for serious topics like retirement or debt.
- Use music licensed for Instagram (from the Edits/IG library) or royalty-free.
- Start the music from the first frame and fade it out over the last second.
- Faceless videos can have music a bit louder (up to about 25%), still under
  the voiceover.

## Ending: CTA overlay

- The last 2 to 3 seconds show a call to action in the same Deco/black style,
  centered.
- Lead-gen / promo: use the user's offer CTA, e.g. `DM me "PLAN"`,
  `Comment "RETIRE" for the guide`, `Book a free call: link in bio`.
- Tips: `Follow for more money tips` or `Save this for later`.
- If the CTA keyword or offer isn't clear, ask the user before finishing.

## Finance content check

These are finance and insurance videos, so before export:

- Flag (don't silently change) any wording that promises results, such as
  "guaranteed returns", "risk-free" or "you will make X%". Let the user decide.
- If the user has a standard disclaimer, add it as small Deco/black text in
  the last few seconds or remind them to put it in the caption.

## Final checklist before export

- [ ] 9:16, everything important inside the safe zone
- [ ] Every text element is Deco font, white on black
- [ ] Centered title for the first 2 seconds
- [ ] Captions on the whole video, 2 to 4 words at a time, spelling checked
- [ ] No pauses over ~0.3s, no filler words, no retakes left in
- [ ] Visual change every 1 to 3 seconds (cuts, zooms, B-roll, emojis)
- [ ] Music at 10% to 20%, voice clear, music fades at the end
- [ ] CTA overlay in the last 2 to 3 seconds
- [ ] Finance wording flagged if needed

When finished, give the user a short summary: final length, what was cut,
title and CTA used, music chosen, and anything you couldn't do with the
available Edits tools.
