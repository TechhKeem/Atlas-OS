S.M.O.O.T.H. Inc. (Strong Men Overcoming Obstacles Through Hard-work Inc.) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded October 28, 2009 at Morgan State University. It has three college **sections**: Coppin State, Morgan State, and Temple. The national board is **The Inc.** The brand is quiet and neutral. Color comes from the crests and from photos of real members.

## Names and voice

- Write the short name as **S.M.O.O.T.H.**, with periods. In running copy, the first mention is "S.M.O.O.T.H. Inc." On legal documents, receipts, and footers, write the full name: "Strong Men Overcoming Obstacles Through Hard-work Inc." Keep the hyphen in "Hard-work."
- Inside the crest, the word appears as SMOOTH in blackletter. That spelling and type exist only in the crest artwork. Never typeset SMOOTH in blackletter yourself.
- Call chapters **sections**, the board **The Inc.**, and members **brothers** in internal copy. The youth program is **Jr. SMOOTH**. The symposium is **R.U.L.E.S. for Black Men**. The October celebration is **SMOOTH Week**.
- The motto is "In every obstacle lies equal opportunity." Use it as a headline or sign-off and never paraphrase it.
- Write short, declarative sentences with a dignified, direct, community-first tone. Example headlines: "Three campuses. One brotherhood." and "17 years down. 100 more to go."
- Use "we" for the organization and "you" for the reader. Use sentence case for headings and buttons. No emoji and no exclamation marks in headlines.
- Say "young men of color" or "young Black and brown men." Don't use deficit language like "at-risk" or "troubled."

## Logos

- `crest-national.png` (navy and silver) is the organization's mark. Use it for anything national or from The Inc.: the website, letterhead, donation receipts, and social avatars.
- `crest-csu.png`, `crest-msu.png`, and `crest-tu.png` belong to their sections. Use a section crest only on that section's materials, and never two section crests side by side without the national crest leading.
- Keep clear space around the crest equal to the height of the ribbon. The minimum size is 48px tall on screens and 0.6 in. in print.
- The crests work on `bg`, `bg-2`, `navy`, `dark`, and their own section color. Never place them on a busy photo without a solid field behind them.
- Don't recolor, stretch, outline, add effects to, or redraw a crest. Don't separate the ribbon from the shield.

## Color

- The site is neutral. Use `bg` and `bg-2` for alternating bands, `ink` for text, and `muted` for eyebrows and captions.
- `navy` is the only S.M.O.O.T.H. color in the interface. Use it for primary buttons, selected states, and the national crest. Text on it is `on-navy`.
- Use `link` for text links and the focus ring. The focus ring is a solid 3px `link` outline, which is 3:1 or better on `bg` and `bg-2` in both themes.
- Section colors (`csu-gold` and `csu-blue`, `msu-blue` and `msu-orange`, `tu-red` and `tu-white`) appear only on that section's own tile, swatch, or materials. Never use them for page backgrounds, buttons, or body text.
- Each section color's usage note gives its contrast limits. White text works on `msu-blue` and `csu-blue`. On `csu-gold`, use `ink` text. On `msu-orange` and `tu-red`, use graphics and large type only.
- Use `dark` for one full-bleed moment per page, such as the Donate band.

## Type

- Set everything in `sans`, the system font stack, which shows SF Pro on Apple devices. Use bold weights for headlines and regular for body text.
- Scale: `hero` for the page's opening line, `headline` for band titles, `stat` for animated numbers, `title` for card names, `subhead` under headlines, `eyebrow` above them, `body` for running text, and `caption` for labels.
- Keep headlines to about eight words or fewer, with tight letter spacing and balanced line breaks. Keep body text to about 65 characters per line.

## Layout and shape

- Use generous whitespace: `band` padding top and bottom, and `space-l` between a headline and its content. Keep the `gutter` on the sides at every width.
- Use one large image per band. Photos use `radius-media`, portraits and bento cards use `radius-card`, fields use `radius-field`, and buttons use `radius-pill`.
- Use no shadows on cards, except a soft one on a section tile while it's hovered. Separate bands with color (`bg` and `bg-2`), not borders.

## Photography

- Use real S.M.O.O.T.H. members and events only, never stock photos.
- Founder and officer portraits are black and white, cropped 4:5 from mid-chest to just above the head, at 800 × 1000px or larger.
- Event and program photos are full color, high resolution, and candid. Vertical (3:4) for program cards, wide (16:9 or wider) for heroes.
- Photos of Jr. SMOOTH youth need written parent consent before they are published.

## Motion

- Sections rise in gently as they scroll into view, large photos ease from slightly smaller to full size, and the stats count up once. One motion per element, nothing loops.
- Respect reduced-motion settings: all motion turns off and content stays fully visible.
