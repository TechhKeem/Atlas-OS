## Disclaimer (on the end card and in the caption)
Hypothetical example for illustration only. Not investment advice. Returns are not guaranteed and do not represent any specific investment. Guaranteed income amounts depend on age, product and insurer; guarantees are backed by the claims-paying ability of the issuing insurer. Lifetime income purchases may have limited liquidity and may not leave a balance to heirs.

## Math check (hypothetical)
- 20 yearly returns: −15, −10, −5, 12, 18, 8, 15, 6, −4, 20, 10, 9, 14, −8, 16, 11, 7, 13, 5, 10 (%) → arithmetic average 6.6%.
- Spending: $40,000 in year 1, +2% each year, taken at the start of the year. Total spending over 20 years for both: **$971,895**.
- James: $1M all invested → $623,261 after year 3 → **$932,053** after year 20.
- James, same returns in reverse (good years first) → **$1,743,320**.
- Carol: $500K buys $35,000/yr fixed lifetime income (7% payout, illustrative); $500K invested covers the rest of the spending (≈$5,000/yr rising) → **$1,066,627** invested after year 20, plus the $35K/yr continuing. (Her $500K annuity premium has no remaining balance in this illustration.)
- Gap shown in the hook: $1,066,627 − $932,053 ≈ **$135,000**.
