## Math check (hypothetical)
- Both spend a flat $60,000/yr, taken at the start of each year (no raises, to keep it simple).
  - James: all $60,000 from $1,000,000 in VOO (6%).
  - Carol: $500,000 private pension account at 6.5% = $32,500/yr for life; $27,500/yr from $500,000 in VOO.
- Same bad start for both: −15%, −10%, −5%. James after year 3: **$574,845**. Carol's VOO after year 3: $293,752.
- Strong market — 20 yearly returns (%): −15, −10, −5, 14.8, 20.8, 10.8, 17.8, 8.8, −1.2, 22.8, 12.8, 11.8, 16.8,
  −5.2, 18.8, 13.8, 9.8, 15.8, 7.8, 13.2 (avg 9.0%).
  - After 20 years: James **$859,271**; Carol VOO **$605,761** + $32,500/yr continuing.

  (avg 6.6%); years 21–30 repeat years 4–13 of that list (12, 18, 8, 15, 6, −4, 20, 10, 9, 14).
  - After 20 years: James $239,850; Carol VOO $247,175.
  - James's account is empty in **year 26**. Carol's VOO at year 30: **$187,362**, and the $32,500/yr continues.
